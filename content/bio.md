I am a PhD student in Linguistics (Psycholinguistics) at the Australian National University, School of Linguistics.

Prior to this, I obtained my MA in Linguistics and Applied Linguistics with a focus on Neurolinguistics from the College of Chinese Language and Culture, Jinan University (GPA: 92/100). I also hold a BA in Teaching Chinese as a Second Language from the same institution (GPA: 89.2/100).

My current research focuses on the neural mechanisms of statistical learning, specifically the neuro-behavioral correlates of auditory and visual statistical learning in language comprehension. I use EEG/ERP techniques (EEGLAB, Fieldtrip) combined with behavioral paradigms and computational approaches (Python, R, MATLAB) to investigate how the brain processes statistical regularities across modalities.
